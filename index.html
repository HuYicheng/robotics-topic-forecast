Robotics Topic Forecast

Which machine-learning and vision topics will grow in robotics papers in 2027? ML & CV venues tend to move first. This page uses last year's change at NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, ICCV and ECCV to forecast next year's change at ICRA, IROS, RSS, CoRL, T-RO, IJRR and Science Robotics, and shows how well that worked in the years we can check.
